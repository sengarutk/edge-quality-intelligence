# Rerun on the official MVTec AD release (for the companion Paper B)

The earlier results used a local `data/mvtec_ad` in which `grid` and `leather` were synthetic
256x256 stand-ins generated by the old `scripts/download_dataset.py`, and `bottle` had an extra
`test/defect` folder. All seven categories now link to the official archive
(MD5 `eefca59f2cede9c3fc5b6befbfec275e`, checked by `scripts/download_dataset.py --verify-only`),
and every result, table, figure and macro was regenerated with
`scripts/reproduce_all_paper_results.sh`.

The structure of `results/ablation/ablation_summary.json` and `paper/tables/ablation.tex` is
unchanged. New outputs live in separate files (`results/short_defect_recall.json`).

## Short defects (new, `scripts/run_short_defect_recall.py`)

Persistence is 4-of-10 (k = 4, N = 10), not 3-of-5. Mean recall over 21 units, 20 defects of
fixed length per 9,000-frame run:

| Length (frames) | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5 | 6 | 8 | 10 | >= 15 |

| B0 | 0.96 | 0.99 | 1.00 | 1.00 | 1.00 | 1.00 | 1.00 | 1.00 | 1.00 |

below 0.95.
